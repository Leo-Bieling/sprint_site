How a sprint works

  1. Free intro call. A 20-minute call to check the sprint fits your situation.
  2. Written proposal and terms. We confirm the scope and the fixed price in writing, with our terms.
  3. 50% deposit. Once you accept, we invoice 50% of the fee, in GBP, and you send us your plans, particulars or site details.
  4. Delivery and balance. Target delivery for every sprint: 10 working days from the Start Date, the day we have both your deposit (in cleared funds) and all the information listed in your proposal. You receive the fixed deliverables and a clear recommendation on what to do next. The balance is invoiced on delivery.

FAQ

  • What is a sprint? A short, fixed-price piece of architect-led work with a defined scope and set deliverables, aimed at one decision.
  • Is the price fixed? Yes. We confirm the scope and fixed price in a written proposal before you commit. Anything outside it, such as a measured survey, is quoted separately in your proposal.
  • Do you do the fit-out or build? No. If you want blng studio to carry on with design, planning or client-side oversight after a sprint, that's a separate appointment.
  • Where do you work? Sites in the UK. The Nursery & School Premises Sprint's regulatory content covers England only; other UK nations are by agreement in the proposal.
  • Who are your clients? Most of our sprints are for businesses and organisations. The Portfolio Review Sprint is for individuals: graduates, Part 1 and Part 2 students and early-career architects. We don't currently offer sprints for homeowners. If you're a founder who hasn't set up a company yet, we can still work with you as a business customer.

The studio

blng sprint is run by blng studio, a principal-led architecture practice in London that works internationally.

The studio designs architecture, interiors and spatial identity, for new buildings, retrofit and adaptive reuse. Its work includes schools and other education buildings, offices and workplaces, housing, and cultural and commercial projects.

It works from the first question to the final detail: early feasibility and site capacity studies, full design and delivery as lead architect, and client-side design advice on contractor-led projects. The practice is rooted in a family practice founded in 1955, and carries forward its care for craft and precise delivery.

A sprint is the first step of that work, the brief, the building check or the capacity study, packaged as a fixed-price piece of work for one decision. The blng studio team carries out every sprint.

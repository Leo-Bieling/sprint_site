The decision: What office do we need, and what will the fit-out cost, before we talk to fit-out contractors?

A fit-out is a large commitment to make without a brief

Growing companies often take an office before they've agreed what they need from it. The landlord's Cat A finish and your own Cat B fit-out get confused, budgets are set too low, and the first detailed layout may come from a contractor who hopes to win the works. A short, architect-led brief puts you in control before you sign or tender.

We don't carry out fit-out works. Our brief sets out what you need, so you can compare contractors' proposals on the same basis.

Cat A vs Cat B, in brief
  • Cat A is the landlord's base finish of the space, typically ceilings, floors, lighting and basic building services.
  • Cat B is your own fit-out: partitions, meeting rooms, kitchen, furniture, IT and brand. It is normally the occupier's cost and the part most open to choice.
  • What's included in "Cat A" varies by building and by lease, so the sprint checks it for the floor you're considering.

What you get

At a fixed price:

  1. Requirements brief: your teams, ways of working, rooms and special requirements, written up as a brief you can hand to landlords, agents and contractors.
  2. Hybrid headcount and space model: how much space you need for the way you actually use the office.
  3. Cat A / Cat B budget range for the fit-out, indicative. We state the source and date of the cost data behind it.
  4. One test-fit on a shortlisted floor, showing whether your brief fits.
  5. Landlord-question list: what to ask about the Cat A specification, services and terms before you commit.

Add-on (optional): Design Guardian review of a contractor's design-and-build proposal against your brief, quoted on request.

FAQ

When should we do this?

Ideally before you sign a lease or ask contractors for proposals, while you still have choices.

Do we need drawings?

The agent's or landlord's floor plans are usually enough for a test-fit.

Will you manage the fit-out?

We don't carry out the works. blng studio can design the Cat B fit-out, or act as your Design Guardian while a contractor does, as a separate appointment.

Is the budget range reliable?

It's an indicative planning range, not a quote. We state the source and date of the cost data behind it.

How does invoicing work?

After the free intro call we send a written proposal and our terms. When you accept, blng studio invoices a 50% deposit, and the balance on delivery. Invoices are issued through Xero, in GBP.

Who does the work?

The blng studio team; our architects carry out every sprint.

Can I cancel?

Yes. If you cancel after accepting the proposal but before work starts, we refund your deposit in full. Once work has started, you pay only for the work done (the fee ÷ 10 × the working days worked), set against your deposit, and we refund any difference. If the work done comes to more than the deposit, you pay the difference, up to the full fee. Full details are in our terms. Work starts on the later of the day your deposit clears and the day we receive all the information listed in your proposal.

The decision: Will this building work as a nursery or school, before you sign the lease?

Gross floor area doesn't tell you capacity, and capacity drives revenue

A building can look big enough and still not work. The EYFS space standards count usable play space only, and the number of children you can register drives your income. Planning, escape routes, toilets and the time it takes to register all affect whether the numbers stack up. Once the lease is signed, rent and fit-out costs start before any fees come in.

More
  • Indoor space per child (EYFS, from 1 Sep 2026): 3.5 m² per child under 2; 2.5 m² per 2-year-old; 2.3 m² per child aged 3 to 5. Storage, thoroughfares, staff areas, cloakrooms, utility rooms, kitchens and toilets don't count. Outdoor space can't be used to increase the number of children. (EYFS framework, paras 3.83–3.84)
  • Planning: day nurseries fall in Class E(f). A move within Class E, for example from office or shop to nursery, isn't development. Planning conditions on earlier permissions, the lease user clause and external works such as plant, fences, canopies and play areas can still stand in the way or need consent. Buildings in other uses need full planning permission. (Use Classes Order; gov.uk)
  • The Class T trap: since 1 August 2021, Class T prior approval applies only to state-funded schools. Registered nurseries can't use it, though some council pages haven't caught up. (GPDO Sch. 2 Pt 3; SI 2021/814)
  • Schools: schools are Class F.1(a), so a change from Class E needs planning permission. Independent school premises must meet Part 5 of the Independent School Standards, and Ofsted inspects the premises before registration. (ISS Part 5; DfE registration guidance)
  • Registration time: Ofsted aims to complete nursery registration within 12 weeks of accepting an application. (Ofsted registration)

What you get

All for one building, at a fixed price:

  1. Planning desk check: use class, planning history, conditions and Article 4 directions as far as they are publicly available, plus the borough's childcare sufficiency report.
  2. Plan review of the drawings you provide. A measured survey is available as an extra, quoted separately in your proposal.
  3. Room-by-room EYFS capacity in age-mix options, so you can see the places for each age group.
  4. One or two test-fit layouts showing how the rooms could be arranged.
  5. Outline fire and escape brief: the escape-route and upper-floor questions to resolve, ready for your fire risk assessor and Building Control.
  6. Works scope with an indicative cost range. We state the source and date of the cost data behind it.
  7. Lease asks: the clauses to ask the landlord for, such as user clause, rent-free period and a break tied to registration.
  8. Ofsted-readiness programme: the premises steps lined up against the registration timeline.
  9. Go/no-go memo: a short written recommendation, with the reasons and the risks.

School / SEND / AP edition: the same structure, checked against ISS Part 5 and the DfE area guidelines (BB103 for mainstream, BB104 for SEND and AP), with BB100 for fire design, and timed to the DfE pre-registration inspection.

What it isn't
  • Not planning advice for a specific site until we've reviewed it. Nothing on this page or in the free checklist is advice for your building. The desk check gives our view of the likely planning route; it isn't a planning application, a lawful development certificate or a guarantee of what the council will decide.
  • Not a fit-out contract. We don't build or sell fit-out works, and the sprint isn't a contractor's quote.
  • Not a fire risk assessment. Your premises will need one under the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005. The sprint gives you an outline brief for it.
  • Not a registration service. We don't apply to Ofsted or the DfE for you, and no one can guarantee registration.
  • Not a measured survey, structural survey or legal review of the lease. Your solicitor and surveyor still have their roles.
  • Not full design. Design, planning applications and Building Regulations drawings are a separate appointment with blng studio.

FAQ

Do I need to have found a building?

Yes. The sprint looks at one specific building, ideally before you agree heads of terms. If you're opening a nursery and still looking, start with the free nursery checklist and capacity calculator.

What do I need to send?

Whatever you have: agent's particulars, floor plans, the draft heads of terms, your planned age mix and opening date. If there are no usable plans, we can arrange a measured survey as an extra, quoted separately in your proposal.

Will you tell me whether I need planning permission?

We'll give you our view of the likely route and what could need consent, based on a desk check. For certainty you may still need a formal route such as a lawful development certificate or a planning application.

How accurate is the cost range?

It's a range for the works scope we identify, meant to help you decide and negotiate, not a contractor's price. It's indicative, and we state the source and date of the cost data behind it.

Does this guarantee Ofsted or DfE registration?

No. It checks the premises against the published requirements and plans the premises side of your timeline. Registration decisions are Ofsted's and the DfE's.

Is this a fire risk assessment?

No. You'll need a fire risk assessment from a competent person. The sprint flags the escape-route and layout questions early and gives that person a brief.

Can you design and manage the fit-out afterwards?

blng studio can carry on with design, planning, Building Regulations and client-side oversight of the contractor, as a separate appointment. We don't carry out the works.

Do you work outside London?

This sprint's regulatory content covers England only. Other UK nations are by agreement in the proposal. Travel outside London is quoted in your proposal. Our other sprints are for sites in the UK.

I haven't set up my company yet. Can I still book?

Yes. If your company isn't set up yet, we can contract with you as a business customer, and the contract moves to your company once it's incorporated. When you accept the proposal, you confirm that you're acting for your business.

Who does the work?

The blng studio team; our architects carry out every sprint.

Can I cancel?

Yes. If you cancel after accepting the proposal but before work starts, we refund your deposit in full. Once work has started, you pay only for the work done (the fee ÷ 10 × the working days worked), set against your deposit, and we refund any difference. If the work done comes to more than the deposit, you pay the difference, up to the full fee. Full details are in our terms. Work starts on the later of the day your deposit clears and the day we receive all the information listed in your proposal.

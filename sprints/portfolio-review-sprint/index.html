Who it's for

  • Graduates applying for their first job in practice.
  • Part 1 and Part 2 students looking for a year-out placement or their next position.
  • Early-career architects applying for a new role.

The decision: Which portfolio do I send, and what should I change first, before I apply?

Why a review with Ariadna

Most portfolios are judged in the first minute: what reviewers see on the first pages decides whether they read on.

Ariadna López Bieling is a Principal of blng studio. Before co-founding the studio she worked at Heatherwick Studio, and she has tutored at the Architectural Association, so she knows what students go through and what practices look for. Across the studio, our experience also includes Zaha Hadid Architects, teaching at the Bartlett (UCL) and workshops at ETH Zurich. The review is ours; it isn't connected with those practices or schools.

The review is feedback on your portfolio. It isn't a guarantee of a job or an interview.

What you get

For £70 incl. VAT: a 30-minute video call with Ariadna, focused on your portfolio and the practices you're applying to, with live, direct feedback on what to change first.

How it works

  1. Book and pay for your call. Pick a time; the £70 incl. VAT is paid when you book.
  2. Have your portfolio open: whatever version you would send with an application. There's no need to send us anything beforehand.
  3. Share your screen on the call.
  4. Live feedback. Ariadna walks through your portfolio with you and tells you directly what to change first.
What we look at
  • Structure: the order of your projects and how the portfolio opens and ends.
  • Project selection: which projects to lead with, which to cut, and what's missing for the practices you're targeting.
  • Narrative: whether each project's story is clear: the question, your idea and your role.
  • Layout: hierarchy, image choice, text and consistency across pages.
  • Length: what to keep for the application and what to save for the interview.
  • The first minute: what a reviewer takes from your first pages, and whether it makes them read on.
What it isn't
  • Not a job guarantee. It's feedback to make your portfolio stronger; the hiring decision is the practice's.
  • Not a redesign. We don't lay out or edit your portfolio for you; you make the changes.
  • Not a recruitment service. We don't put you forward to practices.

FAQ

Do I need to send my portfolio beforehand?

No. You share your screen during the call and Ariadna reacts to your portfolio live.

What format should my portfolio be in?

Any format you can share on screen. A PDF is fine: ideally the version you would send with an application.

Can I bring a portfolio that's still in progress?

Yes. An unfinished portfolio is often the best time for a review, while changes are easy to make.

Who reviews my portfolio?

Ariadna López Bieling, Principal of blng studio.

Will this get me a job?

We can't promise that, and no one honestly can. The review shows you what to change first so your portfolio gives you the best chance with the practices you're applying to.

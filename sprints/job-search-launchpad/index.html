Who it's for

Graduates, Part 1 and Part 2 students and early-career architects who aren't sure where to start their job search. If you're about to rewrite your portfolio or send another application, the workbook helps you take stock first. It's reflection, not careers advice: you do the thinking, in your own words.

What's inside

  • A short workbook for emerging architects, about 30–45 minutes.
  • Three copy-and-paste prompts that work in any chat assistant.
  • The assistant asks you questions. It doesn't write for you or make anything up.
  • You write your answers in your own words.
  • 15 A4 pages to print or fill in by hand.

What you come away with

  • See what you're working with: a plain snapshot of your projects, experience, interests, feedback and constraints.
  • Notice what draws you in: two or three possible directions, written as ideas to test, not decisions.
  • Know your next step: three clear questions to investigate and one small action to take this week.

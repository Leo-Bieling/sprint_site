Site first look: the one-hour desktop check before you offer

  • Developers

A 2-page, one-hour desktop check of a site for new homes in England, using free public maps and registers. Written by blng studio.

What's in the site first look

You need the address, a rough red line and an hour. Mark each line G (no issue found), A (needs a closer look) or R (could stop or reshape the scheme).

  • The site and its history: the title, the site area and the planning history.
  • Constraints on the map: conservation areas, listed buildings, protected trees, Green Belt, flood zones and protected sites.
  • The planning route: the size thresholds, permitted development, permission in principle and talking to the council early.
  • Height and fire: what changes at 11 m and 18 m for flats.
  • Nature and biodiversity net gain: when it applies and the main exemptions.
  • Homes standards and charges: space and access standards, and the Community Infrastructure Levy.

Format: PDF, two pages

Who it's for

  • Landowners, small developers and investors looking at a site for new homes in England, before an offer.

This is a first look from public sources, not a feasibility study: it tells you which questions to answer before you offer.

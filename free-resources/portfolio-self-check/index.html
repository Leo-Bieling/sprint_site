Before you send it: the free portfolio self-check

  • Emerging Architects

16 yes/no questions to run through before you send your portfolio with a job application. One page. Written by blng studio.

What's in the self-check

16 questions to tick as Yes or Not yet, on one page.

  • The first minute: your strongest project on the opening spread, your name and contact details clear, what you're good at obvious within a minute.
  • Project selection and order: your best projects, not all of them, in a deliberate order that shows the range the role needs.
  • Your role: what you did on each project, including group and office work.
  • Narrative: the question or brief, the idea in one sentence and one image, and how it developed.
  • Layout and hierarchy: one clear lead image per spread, short readable text, consistent styles.
  • Length: a short application version, with a longer version kept for the interview.
  • Tailored to the practice: selection, order, format and length adjusted for each application.
  • The file: a single PDF, under about 10 MB, with a sensible file name.

Format: PDF, one page

Who it's for

  • Graduates applying for their first job in practice.
  • Part 1 and Part 2 students looking for a year-out placement or their next position.
  • Early-career architects applying for a new role.

Architect-led sprints, from the blng studio team

blng sprint is a shop by blng studio, a London-based architecture practice working internationally. Each sprint is a fixed-price piece of our architectural work, packaged for one decision and carried out by our architects.

Fixed scope, fixed price

Founders, companies and developers often need an architect's answer to one question before a large commitment: will this building work, what should our office brief say, what can this site hold. A sprint answers that question with a defined scope and a fixed price, confirmed in writing before you commit. We are architects, not contractors.

The team

The whole blng studio team works on the sprints, and our architects carry out the work.
